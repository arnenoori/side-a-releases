Set up Side A Remote

See and answer your Mac’s Claude Code and Codex sessions from your iPhone.

Side A on macOS 14 or later. Side A Remote on iOS 26 or later.

Pair your iPhone

  1. In Side A on your Mac, open Settings › Remote. It checks your Mac and shows what’s left.
  2. Turn on Side A Remote, then click Pair iPhone… to show a pairing code. If macOS asks whether Side A may accept incoming connections, click Allow. That’s your iPhone connecting.
  3. On your iPhone, open Side A Remote, tap Pair with Your Mac and scan the code. Scanning it with the Camera works too.
  4. Check that both devices show the same 6-digit code, then click Pair on your Mac.
  5. Optional: run /hooks once in Codex, and keep your Mac awake while agents work. Both are in Settings › Remote.

Where it connects

Choose in Settings › Remote › Connection on your Mac:

  • Tailscale. Install Tailscale on your Mac and your iPhone and sign in to the same account on both. Works wherever both are online.
  • Also on this Wi-Fi. Connects directly when your iPhone is on the same Wi-Fi as your Mac.
  • Reach this Mac from anywhere. Connects through our relay, end to end encrypted, when your iPhone can’t reach your Mac directly.

If it doesn’t connect

The app says what’s wrong in one line. Here’s what each one means.

Can’t reach your Mac. It may be asleep or Side A is closed.
Wake the Mac and open Side A. Away from your Wi-Fi, check that both devices have Tailscale turned on, or turn on Reach this Mac from anywhere.
Tailscale is off on this iPhone
Open Tailscale on your iPhone and turn it on.
This iPhone was removed from your Mac
Click Pair iPhone… in Settings › Remote on your Mac and scan the new code.
Your Mac has a new security key, so this iPhone has to pair again.
Tap Pair Again and scan the new code on your Mac.
Update Side A on your Mac
Choose Check for Updates… in Side A’s menu.
Update Side A Remote to use it with your Mac
Update the app from TestFlight or the App Store.

Still stuck? In the app, Settings › Test Connection stops at the first step that fails and says what to fix.

Privacy

Your sessions go between your iPhone and your Mac, encrypted with a key from pairing. Notifications are encrypted on your Mac and pass through our relay unreadable. No account, no analytics. Privacy policy

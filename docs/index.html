Setup

  1. Drag Side A to Applications.
  2. Open the lid. Sign in.
  3. Choose a project. Press play.
Requirements

macOS 14+. Apple Silicon or Intel.
Python 3.9+ and Claude Code or Codex CLI.

For sessions started in Side A. Codex and Auto flip are experimental.

Privacy

Your profiles stay on your Mac. Claude and Codex handle sign-in and requests under their own policies.

Anonymous page, download-click, and demo statistics via PostHog. No cookies or recordings. Your browser’s privacy signals are respected. This page checks GitHub for downloads.
